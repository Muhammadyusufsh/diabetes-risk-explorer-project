# Ethics and limitations (working notes)

- The dataset is de-identified and public, but health data is still sensitive in context. Do not try to identify respondents.
- The target represents a self-reported survey status, not a clinical diagnosis.
- Correlation is not causation: a predictive association does not show that a factor causes diabetes.
- The source survey population and its year may limit generalizability to other countries or current populations.
- A model can make unequal errors across demographic groups. We will check this only as an aggregate analytical exercise and will not use the model for individual decisions.
- This project is educational and cannot provide medical advice, screening, triage, or diagnosis.
